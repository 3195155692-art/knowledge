McDonnell 基金会等资助。）

# 精读报告 01：Cognitive Motor Dissociation in Disorders of Consciousness

## 1. 文献信息

- **题目**：Cognitive Motor Dissociation in Disorders of Consciousness
- **作者**：Yelena G. Bodien, Judith Allanson, Paolo Cardone, et al.
- **期刊**：*New England Journal of Medicine*
- **年份/卷期/页码**：2024; 391(7): 598–608
- **DOI**：10.1056/NEJMoa2400645
- **研究类型**：多中心观察性研究 / 临床神经科学研究
- **核心关键词**：cognitive motor dissociation, disorders of consciousness, EEG, fMRI, covert consciousness

## 2. 一句话结论

这项多中心研究最重要的发现是：**在临床上对指令没有可观察行为反应的意识障碍患者中，约 25% 仍可通过 EEG 和/或任务态 fMRI 检测到与“理解指令并执行意图”一致的脑活动，即 cognitive motor dissociation（CMD）**。这直接说明“没有动作反应”不能等同于“没有认知/意识”。

## 3. 为什么这篇文章重要

传统意识障碍评估高度依赖行为，例如：
- 格拉斯哥昏迷评分 GCS：睁眼反应 E；言语反应 V；运动反应 M
- ![手把手教你 Glasgow 昏迷量表评估 - 丁香园](https://img1.dxycdn.com/2021/0924/893/1213163045359375053-44.jpg)

问题在于，严重脑损伤患者可能存在：

> **认知能力仍然存在，但输出通道已经损坏。**

因此，患者可能“知道自己被要求做什么”，但不能把这个意图转化为动作。

CMD 的核心就是：

> **cognition preserved / motor output unavailable**

定义：CRS-R 无命令跟随和可理解言语，即听觉子量表 <3、视觉 <5、口运动/言语 <3、交流 <1，同时至少一个任务态 fMRI 或 EEG 阳性。任务包括运动想象、视觉/听觉辨别等，要求持续数分钟。健康对照中 70%—100% 可出现阳性，说明方法有验证基础。

这实际上与 BCI 的基本思想高度一致：**把脑内意图绕过受损的运动输出通路，直接传递给外部设备。**

## 4. 研究设计

研究汇总了 **353 名**存在意识障碍的成年人。2006—2023 年、6 个国际中心的前瞻性多中心队列研究

其中：

- 行为评估用 CRS-R，评估者盲于 fMRI/EEG 结果
- fMRI/EEG 分析尽量自动化，减少主观偏倚
- 行为评估与任务态检查多在 7 天内，多数在同一天或次日
- 只取第一天最佳表现
- 241 名患者在传统行为评估中没有对指令产生可观察反应；
- 研究者进一步使用任务态 EEG 和/或 fMRI；
- 通过预先定义的指令任务寻找 covert command following。

核心逻辑不是简单测“脑有没有活动”，而是：

> 给患者一个需要持续执行的心理任务 → 检测是否出现与该任务一致、可重复的神经反应。

例如任务可以要求患者进行运动想象。若患者虽然不能动，但其脑活动呈现出与正常受试者执行相同心理任务时相似的模式，就可支持 CMD。

## 5. 主要结果

在 241 名没有可观察指令反应的患者中：

- **60/241 = 25%** 检测到 CMD；
- 其中：
  - 11 名通过 fMRI；
  - 13 名通过 EEG；
  - 36 名通过 EEG + fMRI。

相比之下，对于本身存在可观察指令反应的患者，任务态 fMRI/EEG 出现对应反应的比例更高。

研究还发现 CMD 与以下因素相关：

- 年龄更年轻；
- 受伤时间更长；
- 创伤性脑损伤作为病因。

这些关联属于观察性结果，不能直接解释为因果关系。

## 6. EEG 与 fMRI 各自解决什么问题

### EEG

优势：

- 时间分辨率高；
- 可床旁使用；
- 成本和可及性相对较好；
- 更接近未来临床 BCI。

局限：

- 信号容易受伪迹影响；
- 严重脑损伤患者 EEG 背景异常；
- 个体差异大；
- 单一 EEG 方法可能漏检部分 CMD。

### fMRI

优势：

- 空间分辨率高；
- 可以直接观察任务相关脑网络；
- 经典的 covert command-following 研究基础较强。

局限：

- 设备昂贵；
- 患者转运困难；
- 不适合所有重症患者；
- 时间分辨率和实时性不如 EEG。

因此，一个重要趋势是：

> **多模态 EEG + fMRI 比单模态检测更有可能提高 covert consciousness 的检出率。**

## 7. 这是不是证明患者“有意识”？

不能简单这么说。

更准确的术语是：

> **cognitive motor dissociation**

CMD 说明患者存在某种与指令理解、任务执行和认知加工一致的脑活动。

但：

**CMD ≠ 完整意义上的主观意识被直接测量。**

这是后续 neural decoding 文献继续推进的关键问题。

可以把证据层级理解为：

```text
无行为反应
    ↓
脑活动存在
    ↓
任务相关脑活动
    ↓
能够隐蔽执行指令
    ↓
CMD
    ↓
能够解码具体认知内容
    ↓
潜在 conscious content
```

NEJM 这篇文章主要解决的是中间这一层。

## 8. 与 BCI 的直接关系

这项研究实际上可以看作：

> **“意识评估型 BCI”的临床基础。**

传统 BCI：

```text
患者想象运动
    ↓
EEG
    ↓
classifier
    ↓
光标/机械臂
```

意识障碍患者的 BCI：

```text
患者理解指令
    ↓
运动想象
    ↓
EEG
    ↓
decoder
    ↓
yes/no
```

如果进一步发展：

```text
患者内部认知
    ↓
EEG / fMRI / iEEG
    ↓
neural decoder
    ↓
communication
```

那么 BCI 就从“运动控制工具”变成了“意识检测与交流工具”。

## 9. 文章的主要优势

### 9.1 样本规模大

353 名患者来自多中心数据，是意识障碍领域相当重要的规模。

### 9.2 使用多模态神经信号

不是把 EEG 或 fMRI 当成唯一标准，而是承认不同技术可能检测到不同患者。

### 9.3 临床意义明确

如果患者行为无反应但 CMD 阳性，可能影响撤除生命支持、治疗目标、沟通尝试和患者自主权。但**单次阳性不能直接等同于“有完整意识”或“能决策”**；单次阴性也不能证明无意识。

## 10. 局限

### 10.1 不是所有患者都接受相同检测

EEG、fMRI 和联合检测的患者组成并不完全相同。

### 10.2 CMD 的检测依赖任务设计

如果患者：

- 注意力不足；
- 疲劳；
- 镇静；
- 听觉理解受损；
- 运动想象能力异常；

都可能导致假阴性。

### 10.3 “检测到认知”不等于“重建意识内容”

这也是后续研究最重要的突破口。

## 11. 对 BCI 研究的启示

如果以后做意识 BCI，不建议只设计：

> “有意识 / 无意识”二分类。

更值得研究的是：

### Level 1：state decoding
识别：

- UWS；
- MCS；
- CMD；
- LIS。

### Level 2：command decoding
识别：

- 左/右；
- yes/no；
- motor imagery。

### Level 3：semantic decoding
识别：

- 语言；
- 物体；
- 人；
- 自传体信息。

### Level 4：conscious-content decoding
进一步研究：

> 患者正在体验什么？

## 12. 汇报时建议强调

这篇文章最适合作为汇报的“临床问题起点”：

> **“看不见行为，不代表看不见意识。”**

它把一个临床诊断问题转化成了一个非常典型的 BCI 问题：

> **当运动输出通道失效时，能否绕过运动系统直接读取认知？**

## 13. 参考来源

Bodien YG, et al. *N Engl J Med.* 2024;391:598–608. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2400645.

> ⚠️ 版本提示：本地 PDF 为作者定稿（AAM），与 NEJM 正式排版版（391(7):598–608）在措辞与页码上可能略有差异；正式引用页码时请核对出版社终版。

## A1. Abstract / Background｜问题起点

1. "Patients with brain injury who are unresponsive to commands may perform cognitive tasks that are detected on functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) and electroencephalography (EEG)."
   → 对指令无行为反应的患者，仍可能在 fMRI/EEG 上完成认知任务。
2. "This phenomenon, known as cognitive motor dissociation, has not been systematically studied in a large cohort of persons with disorders of consciousness."
   → 现象早已存在，但缺的是**大样本系统证据**——这就是本文要补的空白。

> **核心论点**：CMD 不是新现象，而是长期缺乏大规模、多中心的系统确认。

## A2. Abstract / Methods｜研究设计

1. "In this prospective cohort study conducted at six international centers, we collected clinical, behavioral, and task-based fMRI and EEG data from a convenience sample of 353 adults with disorders of consciousness."
   → 六中心、前瞻性、353 名成人意识障碍患者。
2. "We assessed the response to commands on task-based fMRI or EEG in participants without an observable response to verbal commands (i.e., those with a behavioral diagnosis of coma, vegetative state, or minimally conscious state–minus) and in participants with an observable response to verbal commands."
   → 关键设计：按“床边有无可观察指令反应”分成两组，组内再看影像学反应。
3. "The presence or absence of an observable response to commands was assessed with the use of the Coma Recovery Scale–Revised (CRS-R)."
   → 行为学基准工具是 CRS-R。

> **核心论点**：用同一套行为学标准（CRS-R）把患者分层，再用 fMRI/EEG 检测“隐蔽的指令跟随”，形成行为×神经的双层判读。

## A3. Abstract / Results｜核心数字

1. "Data from fMRI only or EEG only were available for 65% of the participants, and data from both fMRI and EEG were available for 35%."
   → 多数患者只做了单模态检查，这是检出率的重要限制。
2. "We detected cognitive motor dissociation in 60 of the 241 participants (25%) without an observable response to commands, of whom 11 had been assessed with the use of fMRI only, 13 with the use of EEG only, and 36 with the use of both techniques."
   → **25%**；而且 60% 的阳性来自“双模态”患者。
3. "Cognitive motor dissociation was associated with younger age, longer time since injury, and brain trauma as an etiologic factor."
   → CMD 的相关因素：更年轻、伤后时间更长、创伤性脑损伤。
4. "In contrast, responses on task-based fMRI or EEG occurred in 43 of 112 participants (38%) with an observable response to verbal commands."
   → **对照组也只有 38% 阳性**——这一点常被忽略，它说明范式本身假阴性率很高。

> **核心论点**：25% vs 38% 的对比有两层含义：CMD 并不罕见；但现有任务范式灵敏度有限，阴性不能否定意识。

## A4. Abstract / Conclusions｜作者自述结论

1. "Approximately one in four participants without an observable response to commands performed a cognitive task on fMRI or EEG as compared with one in three participants with an observable response to commands."
   → 一句话结论：1/4 vs 1/3。
2. "(Funded by the James S. McDonnell Foundation and others.)"
   → 非商业资助，由基金会支持。

> **核心论点**：CMD 的检出率已接近“有反应患者”的检出率，提示无反应群体中隐藏着大量有认知能力的人。

## A5. Introduction｜为什么要做

1. "Cognitive motor dissociation is an established phenomenon in which persons with severe brain injury who are behaviorally unresponsive to commands show brain activation on functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) or electroencephalography (EEG) when presented with cognitive tasks, such as motor imagery commands."
   → CMD 的定义句。
2. "The failure to identify cognitive motor dissociation in patients with disorders of consciousness could affect decisions related to the withdrawal of life-sustaining treatment, goals of care, and clinical management."
   → 漏检的后果直接落在**撤除生命支持**这类决策上。
3. "Evidence of cognitive motor dissociation may prompt more thorough investigation of subtle behaviors that are under volitional control, uncovering potential avenues for communicating with the patient and respecting their autonomy."
   → 检出 CMD 会反过来促进寻找细微的自主动作，并打开交流通道。
4. "In previous studies, cognitive motor dissociation was observed in 10 to 20% of persons with a disorder of consciousness; similar findings were observed in persons with acute brain injury or chronic brain injury, as well as across etiologic factors."
   → 既往区间是 10–20%，且跨急性/慢性、跨病因都存在。
5. "Detection of cognitive motor dissociation has been associated with more rapid recovery and better outcomes at 1 year after injury."
   → CMD 与更好的预后相关。
6. "To be detected on fMRI or EEG, responses to commands must be sustained and reflect not only language comprehension but also recruitment of more cognitive resources (e.g., short-term memory, attention, and persistence) than may be required for responding to a single command at the bedside."
   → 影像学任务比床边单次指令**更耗认知资源**，这是范式门槛。
7. "Identifying that a patient who otherwise appears to be unconscious has the capacity for cognitive processing may mitigate emotional harm when the patient's clinical team and family recognize that the patient is aware and treats the patient as such."
   → 检出本身具有伦理与情感价值。
8. "International clinical guidelines vary with regard to recommendations about the use of fMRI and EEG for detecting cognitive motor dissociation, with some guidelines supporting their use and at least one guideline proposing the need for further study before these techniques are routinely used in clinical practice."
   → 指南并不统一——本文正是要把证据等级往上推。

> **核心论点**：CMD 的临床意义不只是分类，而是影响生死决策、预后判断和医患关系；但现有指南缺乏统一依据。

## A6. Methods｜判据与统计

1. "Six multinational sites contributed behavioral and task-based fMRI data, EEG data, or both to a centrally curated Research Electronic Data Capture (REDCap) database from 2006 to 2023."
   → 数据跨度 2006–2023，集中式数据库汇总。
2. "The examiners who administered the CRS-R were unaware of the findings on fMRI and EEG."
   → 行为学评估者对影像结果设盲。
3. "Trained study staff who were unaware of the findings from the behavioral assessment removed artifacts from the EEG data."
   → EEG 去伪迹同样设盲。
4. "Cognitive motor dissociation was operationally defined as a lack of responses to commands and a lack of intelligible speech as assessed with the CRS-R (i.e., a score of <3 on the auditory subscale, <5 on the visual subscale, <3 on the oromotor–verbal subscale, and <1 on the communication subscale) in the context of a response to a task-based fMRI paradigm, a task-based EEG paradigm, or both."
   → **可操作定义**：行为学阴性 + 任务态影像学阳性（含具体分量表阈值）。
5. "The preservation or recovery of multiple complex cognitive functions that are required to perform the tasks assessed with fMRI and EEG over minutes of sustained engagement minimizes the risk of spurious responses on fMRI and EEG."
   → 为什么“持续数十秒的任务”比单次指令更难被伪迹伪造。
6. "Given this context, we interpreted responses on fMRI and EEG in participants without an observable response to commands to be specific for cognitive motor dissociation but at a potential cost of reduced sensitivity."
   → 作者明确交换关系：**高特异度、低灵敏度**。

> **核心论点**：本文的阳性结果可以较放心地当作 CMD；但阴性结果不能当作“无意识”——这是一个刻意选择的方向性偏倚。

## A7. Results｜参与者与检出

1. "The central database included data for 478 participants, of whom 125 ... were excluded from the current study."
   → 478 入池 → 353 入组。
2. "A total of 215 participants (61%) underwent at least one fMRI assessment, and 260 participants (74%) underwent at least one EEG assessment. Both fMRI and EEG were performed in 122 participants (35%), and fMRI only or EEG only were performed in 231 (65%)."
   → EEG 覆盖高于 fMRI；双模态仅 35%。
3. "The CRS-R assessment occurred within a median of 1 day (interquartile range, 0 to 2) of fMRI and 0 days (interquartile range, 0 to 1) before or after EEG."
   → 行为学与影像学评估在时间上高度贴近（±7 天内），减少状态漂移。
4. "Of the 241 participants with a diagnosis of coma or vegetative state (i.e., participants who were unconscious) or minimally conscious state–minus on the basis of the score on the CRS-R, 60 (25%) had a response to commands on task-based fMRI, task-based EEG, or both."
   → 主结果。
5. "The median age was lower among participants with cognitive motor dissociation than among those without cognitive motor dissociation (30.5 years [interquartile range, 22.6 to 43.0] vs. 45.3 years [interquartile range, 26.7 to 59.3]), and a higher percentage of participants with cognitive motor dissociation than without cognitive motor dissociation had brain trauma as an etiologic factor (65% vs. 38%) and a diagnosis of minimally conscious state–minus according to the score on the CRS-R (53% vs. 38%)."
   → CMD 组：更年轻（30.5 vs 45.3）、外伤比例更高（65% vs 38%）、MCS− 更多（53% vs 38%）。
6. "The CRS-R assessment occurred later after brain injury in participants with cognitive motor dissociation than in those without cognitive motor dissociation (10.7 months [interquartile range, 3.7 to 24.3] vs. 4.3 months [interquartile range, 0.1 to 14.4])."
   → 伤后时间更长（10.7 vs 4.3 个月）——与“恢复中”一致。
7. "Among participants with cognitive motor dissociation, 18% were assessed with fMRI only, 22% with EEG only, and 60% with both fMRI and EEG."
   → 单模态会漏掉相当一部分 CMD。
8. "Of the 112 participants with a diagnosis of minimally conscious state–plus or who had emerged from the minimally conscious state as assessed with the CRS-R, 43 (38%) had a response to commands on task-based fMRI, task-based EEG, or both."
   → 有反应组的影像学阳性率仅 38%。
9. "Responses to commands on fMRI and EEG were absent in more than 60% of the participants who had an observable response to commands during assessment with the CRS-R at the bedside."
   → 强有力的假阴性证据：床边明确能配合，影像学却超过 60% 无反应。

> **核心论点**：25% 是“下限而非上限”；同时 38% 这一对照数字证明范式的灵敏度瓶颈真实存在。

## A8. Results｜三种评估之间的一致性

1. "The level of agreement between evidence of a response on the basis of the CRS-R, fMRI, and EEG assessments, as indicated by kappa coefficients, was low."
2. "Kappa coefficients ranged from 0.09 to 0.15 for agreement between evidence of a response according to CRS-R findings and evidence according to fMRI or EEG findings and from 0.02 to 0.04 for agreement between evidence on the basis of fMRI findings and EEG findings."
   → 行为×影像 kappa 仅 0.09–0.15；fMRI×EEG 更低（0.02–0.04）。

> **核心论点**：CRS-R、fMRI、EEG 在测量**同一个人的意识**时几乎不收敛——它们不是同一把尺子的三种刻度，而是三条互补通道。

## A9. Discussion｜与既往研究的差异

1. "we detected cognitive motor dissociation on task-based fMRI or EEG in approximately 25% of the participants. This percentage is higher than previous estimates."
   → 高于既往 10–20%。
2. "the use of both imaging techniques appears to be a more sensitive approach than the use of one of the techniques alone."
   → 双模态更敏感。
3. "The percentage of participants with cognitive motor dissociation may have been even higher if all the participants had been assessed with both imaging techniques."
   → 检出率是被设计“压住”的。
4. "Previous studies have shown that cognitive motor dissociation was most common among participants with traumatic brain injury, those with chronic disorders of consciousness, and those with a behavioral diagnosis of minimally conscious state–minus, findings that were indicated in our study."
   → 与既往一致的三类高发人群。
5. "However, cognitive motor dissociation was also detected in participants with nontraumatic causes of brain injury (e.g., stroke and cardiac arrest), those with acute disorders of consciousness, and those who were behaviorally unconscious, as indicated by a diagnosis of coma or vegetative state."
   → **重要反面**：非创伤、急性期、甚至昏迷/植物状态也能检出 CMD。

> **核心论点**：不能把 CMD 当成“外伤 + 慢性期 + MCS−”的专属现象，否则会系统性漏检。

## A10. Discussion｜为什么会低估

1. "the paradigms used in studies of task-based fMRI and EEG may require more cognitive resources (e.g., short-term memory, selective attention, and mental persistence) than in typical command-following trials performed at the bedside."
   → 范式本身更难。
2. "This hypothesis ... is supported by our finding that responses on fMRI and EEG were detected in only 38% of the participants with an observable response to commands at the bedside."
   → 用对照组阳性率反推范式难度。
3. "the fMRI and EEG analytic techniques used at the study sites were intentionally designed to minimize the potential for a false-positive result, which may increase the likelihood of a false-negative finding."
   → 刻意压低假阳性 → 抬高假阴性。
4. "most studies assess participants with the use of either fMRI or EEG. We found that participants who were assessed with both imaging techniques were more likely to have cognitive motor dissociation than those who were assessed with one technique."
   → 单模态研究天然低估。
5. "behavioral fluctuation is common among patients across all disorders of consciousness, which may contribute to the lack of a response on fMRI or EEG or to disparate findings between these two techniques."
   → 状态波动解释低一致性与漏检。

> **核心论点**：检出率是设计参数的函数（任务难度、统计阈值、模态数量、状态波动），而不是纯粹的生物学常数。

## A11. Discussion｜局限

1. "All the participants with chronic brain injury had survived their initial illness or injury and had access to a research facility with advanced fMRI and EEG capabilities. This survival bias may reflect greater cognitive reserve and resilience over time among the participants."
   → 生存者偏倚：能进研究中心的患者本身更“强”。
2. "participating sites used heterogeneous strategies to acquire, analyze, and interpret data, which led to differences in the number, type, and ordering of the cognitive tasks assessed on fMRI and EEG."
   → 六中心异质性明显。
3. "these differences, along with variations in recruitment strategies and participant characteristics, may have contributed to the unequal percentage of participants with cognitive motor dissociation observed at each site (range, 2 to 45%)."
   → **中心间差异 2%–45%**，是全文最值得警惕的一个数字。
4. "The statistical analyses that were conducted as part of this study were univariate and descriptive. Thus, we were unable to evaluate the independent contribution of any one variable in predicting cognitive motor dissociation."
   → 描述性分析，不能做因果或独立预测推断。
5. "access to both the specially trained personnel and techniques that are needed to assess persons for cognitive motor dissociation is currently available in only a few academic medical centers worldwide, which limits the feasibility of performing these assessments in general practice."
   → 可及性极低。

> **核心论点**：结论方向可信（CMD 不罕见），但具体百分比不可跨中心外推；标准化是落地前提。

## A12. Discussion｜结论

1. "The results of our study, which used neuroimaging and electrophysiological techniques, indicate that cognitive motor dissociation is more common than previously realized."
   → 主结论。
2. "the knowledge that cognitive motor dissociation is not rare should prompt further study to explore whether its detection can lead to improved outcomes."
   → 下一步：检出能否改善结局。
3. "the standardization, validation, and simplification of task-based fMRI and EEG methods that are used to detect cognitive motor dissociation are needed to prompt widespread clinical integration of these techniques and investigation of the bioethical implications of the findings."
   → **标准化、验证、简化**是三个关键词。

> **核心论点**：本文解决的是“有多常见”，没有解决“检出后能不能改变结局”——后者才是临床转化的真正门槛，也是 BCI 方向的机会窗口。
